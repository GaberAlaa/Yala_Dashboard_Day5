Global Death Rates Analysis Dashboard (1970-2010)

This Power BI dashboard visualizes historical global mortality data, tracking the relationship between population growth and death rates across different demographics and time periods.
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Population: 7 billion (at the 2010 data point).
- Annual Mortality: 53 million deaths per year.
- Standardized Rate: 766.24 average deaths for every 100,000 people.

Core Analysis

- Temporal Trends: A dual-axis line chart illustrates the inverse relationship between population growth (upward trend) and the average death rate (downward trend) from 1970 to 2010.
- Age Demographics: A horizontal bar chart identifies the 80+ age group as having the highest mortality rate (11.5K per 100,000), followed by infants under 1 year (3.7K).
- Geographic Distribution: A bubble map visualizes population density and death statistics per country, showing significant data concentrations across Africa, Europe, and Asia.
